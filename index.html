Introduction

Chinese STEM Visual QA (CSVQA) is a high school education benchmark test set for multimodal science reasoning, including visual question answering tasks in four major subjects: physics, mathematics, chemistry, and biology. It aims to evaluate the performance of large-scale visual language models in complex tasks that require rigorous scientific reasoning. CSVQA contains 12 different types of images, including tables, charts, diagrams, flow charts, microscopic images, molecular models, chemical structures, geometric figures, photographs, paintings, mechanical diagrams and circuit diagrams.The dataset contains open-ended and selective questions. Unlike existing benchmark datasets that only focus on basic perception capabilities or low-difficulty text comprehension, CSVQA places special emphasis on the model's recognition, understanding, and reasoning capabilities in science subjects, especially the challenges faced when processing Chinese content.

This benchmark proposes a cross-disciplinary multimodal association reasoning task, requiring the model to collaboratively process heterogeneous modal data in a single problem scenario and establish cross-modal semantic associations. This type of design highly restores the real cognitive process of "experimental data analysis + theoretical derivation and verification" in Chinese high school science education.We evaluated the VLMs that need to be supplemented, and the experimental results show that CSVQA-Hard can effectively distinguish the performance of models in complex scientific reasoning tasks, providing an important reference for the further development of multimodal models in the scientific field.

We plan to release two versions
CSVQA-Hard: is a pilot version of the CSVQA, which contains 30 difficult multi-modal science questions.
CSVQA: the official version of CSVQA, which contains 500 multi-modal questions

Dataset Source

This study uses a dynamic semantic alignment method based on a large language model, which significantly improves data processing efficiency compared to traditional rule matching and ensures high quality and rigor of the data. The exercises in the dataset are all from high school textbooks and college entrance examination questions.

OCR: By structuring the original document and introducing the OCR mechanism, the graphic content is converted into a standardized format. During this process, a labeling team of more than 10 people was established and a double verification mechanism was established.

Semantic alignment: To achieve accurate matching between questions and answers, the team designed an automatic alignment process based on a large language model, used LLM to perform semantic association analysis, and established a preliminary mapping relationship by calculating the similarity of context vectors.

Manual screening: manual verification is used to check the correspondence in complex situations such as multiple pages across topics and mixed text and graphics.

Review output: The final data was produced after three rounds of independent review, including format compliance check (schema validation), content completeness confirmation (completeness of question-answer-image triples), and topic accuracy review (sampling validation).

Key Features

Subject range: covers the four major subjects of physics, chemistry, biology and mathematics, and is completely based on exercises from Chinese high school textbooks.

Question types: including multiple-choice questions and open-ended questions, which test the model's in-depth understanding of scientific knowledge and multi-step reasoning ability.

Diverse images: The dataset contains 12 different types of scientific images, such as function images, circuit diagrams, chemical structures, etc.

Rich analysis: The data contains standard answers and detailed analysis to help the model perform supervised learning and self-supervised learning.

High-quality annotation: All data are proofread by professional annotators to ensure data accuracy.

Model benchmark: Currently, 300 SOTA models have been tested on this dataset and performed excellently.
