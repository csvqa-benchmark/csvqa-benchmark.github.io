Introduction

Chinese STEM Visual QA (CSVQA) is a high school education benchmark test set for multimodal science reasoning, including visual question answering tasks in four disciplines: physics, mathematics, chemistry, and biology. It aims to evaluate the performance of large-scale visual language models in complex tasks that require rigorous scientific reasoning. CSVQA contains 12 different types of images, including tables, charts, diagrams, flowcharts, microscopic images, molecular models, chemical structures, geometric shapes, photos, paintings, mechanics diagrams, and circuit diagrams.The dataset contains open-ended and selective questions. Unlike existing benchmark datasets that only focus on basic perception capabilities or low-difficulty text comprehension, CSVQA places special emphasis on the model's recognition, understanding, and reasoning capabilities in science subjects, especially the challenges faced when processing Chinese content.

This benchmark proposes a cross-disciplinary multimodal reasoning task where models must integrate heterogeneous data modalities and establish cross-modal semantic associations. Such a design closely mirrors the authentic cognitive process of "experimental data analysis + theoretical derivation" in Chinese high school science education.

We plan to release two versions
CSVQA-Hard: is a pilot version of the CSVQA, which contains 30 difficult multi-modal science questions.
CSVQA: the full version of the CSVQA benchmark (coming soon) will further expand to include even more diverse question types and interdisciplinary challenges.

Dataset Source

Our data collection leverages a dynamic semantic alignment approach based on large language models, which significantly improves processing efficiency over traditional rule-based methods while ensuring high quality and rigor. The questions in our dataset are sourced from authoritative high school textbooks and Gaokao (national college entrance exam) papers.

OCR Processing: We begin by structurally processing the original documents and integrating OCR techniques to convert both textual and graphical content into standardized formats. During this stage, a team of over 10 annotators is organized, and a dual verification mechanism is implemented to ensure high extraction accuracy.

Semantic Alignment: To achieve precise matching between questions and answers, our team has designed an automatic alignment pipeline based on large language models. Using LLMs for semantic association analysis, we compute contextual embedding similarities to establish an initial mapping between question texts and candidate answer regions.

Manual Screening: We then conduct thorough manual verification, focusing on complex scenarios such as multi-page questions and mixed text-image layouts, to ensure accurate correspondence between the components.

Multi-round Review and Finalization: The dataset undergoes three independent rounds of review, including format compliance checks (via schema validation), content integrity confirmation (ensuring the completeness of question-answer-image triplets), and subject accuracy audits (through random sampling).

Key Features

Disciplines Covered: Encompasses four major subjects—physics, chemistry, biology, and mathematics—based entirely on high school textbooks.

Question Types: Includes both multiple-choice and open-ended questions, designed to assess in-depth understanding and multi-step reasoning in scientific knowledge.

Diverse Images Types: Contains 12 distinct types of scientific images such as function plots, circuit diagrams, and chemical structures.

Rich Explanations: Each question comes with standard answers and detailed explanations, supporting both supervised and self-supervised learning.

High-Quality Annotation: All data entries are meticulously verified by professional annotators to ensure accuracy.

Model Benchmarking: To date, (to be determined) state-of-the-art models have been evaluated on this dataset.
